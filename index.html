Quick start

  1. 01
    Open a filePress Ctrl + O or drop Markdown and text into the window.
  2. 02
    Choose a modeUse the separate Rendered/Source and Read only/Edit controls.
  3. 03
    Adjust the viewUse the gear for lines, minimap and wrap; use the palette for theme and type.
