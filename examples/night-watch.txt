LUMEN HARBOR  ·  night letter  ·  last watch
26 August 2026, 23:47  ·  fog 62 m  ·  tide flood 184 cm  ·  lamp #4 trimmed

This is a plain-text companion. It has no headings the reader can promote
into an outline. It has no fences for the highlighter. It has sentences,
which is what .txt is for.

I meant to post this in the morning. The morning did not come in on time.
The radio swallowed its own call sign around 22:10 and gave me a number
instead of a voice. I wrote the number on the blotter and then the rain
erased the last digit, which I am choosing to treat as a kindness.

The chair by the glass is empty. It has been empty the entire watch. I
checked twice because the reflection is a good liar. If you are reading
this from a later desk, do not sit down in the window. Sit at the wood.

Ida is on the spine of the blue book again. Percival is drunk on the
kerosene halo and will deny it. Little Noise is a smudge with opinions.
Glass-Eater does not photograph. I counted fourteen moths and then I
counted them again because the fog makes a census feel like guesswork.

I trimmed the wick to 7 mm at 02:10 of the previous cycle and it has held.
Fuel is 390 ml. If you are the first watch, do not "improve" the flame.
The bored lantern is still a lantern.

Things the station is not:
    a cloud
    a product tour
    a place that wants your account
    a grave, even when the radio goes quiet

Things the station is:
    a desk that learned to float
    a file on disk
    a habit of keeping minutes the tide will not keep

Unicode belongs in a letter: café cooling on the sill, a naïve attempt to
sleep sitting up, a note I once started in 東京 and never finished, an em
dash — like this — where a comma would have been more honest.

If you open the other files, they will pretend to be systems. JSON will
count moths as if moths were integers. YAML will indent the watches.
CSV will put salt in columns. The Kodi .nfo will claim this night was a
film. The Windows .nfo will claim the pier was a computer. The Unicode
program .nfo will put a box around the installer joke. Believe the rain
first.

I am leaving the unfinished tasks unfinished. Someone has to.

— the unnamed keeper

Postscript. The cyanotype in the chart locker still says north is a
rumour we agreed to keep. I agree again, in writing, so the rumour has
a witness.

Postscript 2. If this file wraps, the line length was a choice. Seventy
columns is a courtesy, not a vow. The NFO grid is stricter. This letter
is allowed to breathe.
